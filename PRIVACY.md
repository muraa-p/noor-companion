# Privacy Policy

*Effective date: September 10, 2026*

Thank you for using **Noor** (نُور). This Privacy Policy explains how the
application handles your information. The short version: **Noor collects no
personal data. Everything you do stays on your device.**

## 1. Developer
Noor is provided by an independent developer ("we", "us"). If you have any
questions about this policy, contact us at **muraadnagrij@gmail.com**.

## 2. No personal data collection
Noor does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There is
no account system, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no crash
reporting, and no third-party trackers in the app.

We do not access your contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, or location.
The app never requests the location permission.

## 3. Data that stays on your device
The following information is stored **only on your device** and is never sent
to us or to anyone else:

- Your app settings and preferences (theme, theme toggle choice, reminder
  time, selected reciter).
- Qur'an reading progress, bookmarks and last-read positions per surah.
- Your hasanat (good deeds) counter and daily tracking.
- Your custom dhikr entries.
- Any recitation audio files you download for offline listening.

This data is stored on-device by the operating system's local storage. It
cannot be accessed by anyone, including us. Uninstalling the app removes it.
There is no cloud backup of this data.

The only practice a user can adjust: deleting the app or clearing its app data
removes everything described in this section.

## 4. Network requests the app makes
The app is fully usable offline. It makes only the following network requests,
which the user initiates:

1. **Prayer times** — when you open the prayer times screen, the app calls the
   Aladhan prayer-times API (`api.aladhan.com`) with the **city and country you
   typed yourself** to calculate prayer schedules. This request is subject to
   Aladhan's own data practices. As with any web request, the service may
   briefly see your device's IP address at the network-server level; Noor does
   not send it your name, email, or any other identifier.
2. **Quran recitation downloads** — when you voluntarily download a
   recitation, audio files are fetched from the Islamic Network CDN
   (`cdn.islamic.network`). As above, only the standard technical request data
   (e.g., the IP address) is exchanged at the network level.

Neither request involves cookies, advertising identifiers, or tracking.

## 5. Notifications
If you choose to enable the daily reminder, the app asks for the Android
notification permission and schedules the reminder on your device. This is
purely local; the content and schedule of your reminders are never transmitted.

## 6. Children
Noor is a Qur'an reading and reflection app suitable for all ages. Because the
app does not collect any personal information from anyone, we have nothing
relevant to the personal data of children to collect, store, or share.

## 7. Third-party services
The app contains no third-party ad networks or analytic services. The third
party services involved are limited to:

- **Aladhan (api.aladhan.com)** — prayer times calculation.
- **Islamic Network (cdn.islamic.network)** — optional recitation downloads.

We recommend you also review their respective privacy policies, as their
servers process standard technical data (such as IP addresses) when network
requests are made.

## 8. Data security
Since no personal data ever leaves your device, there is no personal data to
protect in transit or at rest on our side. Local data is protected by the
security of the device itself.

## 9. Data retention and deletion
We do not hold any of your data, so there is nothing for us to retain or
delete. You can erase all data stored by the app at any time by uninstalling
Noor or clearing its app data through your device's settings.

## 10. Changes to this policy
If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this
URL and the "Effective date" above will be updated. You are encouraged to
review it occasionally. Continued use of the app after changes take effect
constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
